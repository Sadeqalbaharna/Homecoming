Homecoming · WHOOP integration

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 12 August 2026

Short version: Homecoming uses your WHOOP data only to show your personal fitness information inside your local Homecoming desktop application. It does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with other users.

1. Scope

This policy covers the optional connection between the Homecoming desktop application and a WHOOP account. Homecoming is an independent personal application and is not endorsed by or affiliated with WHOOP, Inc.

2. Information Homecoming accesses

After you explicitly approve access through WHOOP's OAuth consent screen, Homecoming may read:

  • body measurements supplied by WHOOP, including weight;
  • Recovery information, including Recovery score, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability;
  • physiological cycle information, including Strain;
  • sleep information, including sleep duration and performance; and
  • workout information, including whether an activity was recorded.

Homecoming does not request your WHOOP password. Authentication occurs on WHOOP's own authorization page. Homecoming requests read-only scopes and does not modify data in WHOOP.

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to populate the Fitness tracker in your Homecoming desktop UI, display current summaries and maintain your local measurement history. Homecoming does not use WHOOP data for advertising, automated employment or insurance decisions, sale, profiling of other people, or medical diagnosis.

4. Storage and security

OAuth client credentials and WHOOP access and refresh tokens are stored using encrypted operating-system secure storage on the Windows device running Homecoming. Fitness summaries and imported measurements are stored locally on that device with the rest of the personal Fitness tracker record. They are not intentionally uploaded by this integration to Firebase or another Homecoming cloud database.

Data is transferred from WHOOP over encrypted HTTPS connections. No security method can guarantee absolute protection, but Homecoming is designed to minimize access and keep the integration local and read-only.

5. Sharing and sale

Homecoming does not sell, rent, license or share your WHOOP information with advertisers, data brokers, other Homecoming users or unrelated third parties. Information may be disclosed only if required by applicable law or necessary to protect the security and legal rights of the user or developer.

6. Retention, disconnection and deletion

You can select Disconnect + revoke in Homecoming's Fitness tracker. Homecoming will request revocation of its WHOOP access and delete the locally stored OAuth tokens. Previously imported local fitness history is preserved so you do not unexpectedly lose your personal record; you can delete individual entries from the Fitness tracker. You may also revoke the integration from WHOOP's own application.

To request help deleting all locally stored WHOOP-derived fitness information, contact the address below. Removing the Homecoming application and its local application data also removes the local record.

7. Your choices

Connecting WHOOP is optional. You may decline authorization, disconnect at any time, review imported values in the Fitness tracker, and continue using manual Fitness tracker features without WHOOP.

8. Health disclaimer

Homecoming's Fitness tracker is a personal information display, not a medical device or medical record. It does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified professional for health decisions.

9. WHOOP's services

WHOOP processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. This Homecoming policy does not control WHOOP's collection, use or security practices.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated if the integration's data access or behavior changes. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be reflected before broader data access is requested.

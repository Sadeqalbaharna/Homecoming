## Safe arithmetic implications

- Minimum gross venue bill per average full table: `4 × BHD12 = BHD48`.
- Minimum gross venue bill across two average full tables per week:
  `2 × 4 × BHD12 = BHD96`.
- One additional average full table would add at least BHD48 gross venue bill
  before food cost, staff labor, DM compensation, refunds/discounts or other
  operating cost.

These are arithmetic floors, not profit, incremental causal impact, settled-bank
proof or a forecast. Existing table revenue is a baseline, not revenue created
by Find My Table.

## Superseded assumptions

The earlier v2 Blueprint assumed hosted-table demand, venue participation and
the mechanism itself were unverified. Direct sponsor operating evidence
invalidates that premise. The v2 BHD5/BHD7 seat-ticket pilot is historical and
must not govern the active product.

The following remain **UNVERIFIED**:

- average actual spend per player and table;
- food/beverage cost and contribution margin;
- staff and sponsor labor per table;
- how players currently discover the tables;
- unmatched-player volume and reasons;
- fill lead time, cancellation, no-show and repeat rates;
- external DM compensation and availability;
- schedule/fit mismatch frequency;
- maximum sustainable weekly table capacity;
- incremental settled revenue attributable to a future matching system.

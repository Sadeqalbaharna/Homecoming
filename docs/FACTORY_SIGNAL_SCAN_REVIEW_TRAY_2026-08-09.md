## Review tray

### `FSC-20260809-01` — Calm Before You Go

- **Idea:** A printable-and-mobile personal reconnaissance kit that helps a
  sensory-sensitive person preview one specific outing and record what noise,
  lighting, crowds, escape routes, and communication demands to expect.
- **Consumer/pain:** Autistic and otherwise sensory-sensitive adults, plus
  families, who avoid necessary or enjoyable outings because the experience is
  unpredictable and may impair speech or regulation.
- **Audience rationale:** A committed niche repeatedly asks for sensory details;
  public-space research identifies supermarkets, eateries, transport, health
  care, and shops as disabling contexts. The need is real, but a generic map is
  already crowded, so the underserved wedge is a private, venue-specific prep
  ritual that works without community coverage.
- **Evidence:** [Autistic adults’ sensory experiences in public spaces](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10726197/);
  [2025 Design Trust report](https://www.designtrust.org/publications/report-neurodivergence-research-technology/);
  [direct community request for indoor maps and more quiet access](https://www.reddit.com/r/AutisticAdults/comments/1k01syg/).
  Competitor/saturation evidence: [NeuroHub](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/neurohub/id1621792528),
  [SenseMap](https://sensemap.app/), and [Quokalm](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.benjamin.kokoon).
- **Confidence/proof:** MEDIUM; externally evidenced, provisional. Pain and
  crowding are evidenced; willingness to pay for this non-map wedge is not.
- **Simplest buyable product:** A $9 accessible PDF/phone worksheet pack with
  venue-call script, sensory profile, route rehearsal, exit plan, and a reusable
  “what I learned” card—co-designed and tested with neurodivergent adults.
- **Path to banked money:** Sell the download on an existing digital marketplace;
  reconcile a genuine customer order through the processor payout into the bank.
- **Strongest rejection:** Accessibility products designed without paid
  co-design can harm or patronize; free competing apps may erase willingness to
  pay.
- **Fingerprint:** `sensory-preview|private-venue-rehearsal|offline-first|adult`
- **Origin:** `WEB-003` hypothesis “reduce uncertainty before sensory-heavy public outings,” refined after `WEB-007` rejected another crowdsourced map.

### `FSC-20260809-02` — Clear Visit: Menopause Evidence Pack

- **Idea:** A plain-language, non-diagnostic pack that turns two weeks of
  symptoms, sleep, costs, and attempted remedies into a one-page doctor-visit
  brief and a checklist for spotting unsupported product claims.
- **Consumer/pain:** Perimenopausal and menopausal consumers overwhelmed by
  symptoms, fragmented online information, treatment cost, and aggressive
  wellness marketing.
- **Audience rationale:** This is mainstream and demonstrably underserved in
  trustworthy navigation: survey evidence shows sleep disruption and delayed
  treatment due to cost, while current research reports unmet needs in online
  menopause information. The wedge is appointment preparation and claim
  literacy, not another generic tracker or treatment promise.
- **Evidence:** [2025 GoodRx affordability survey](https://www.goodrx.com/healthcare-access/research/menopause-survey-affordability);
  [2025 study of unmet online information needs](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41032866/);
  [NielsenIQ underserved-market report](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/report/2025/hot-flashes-cold-shoulders-the-underserved-womens-health/).
  Saturation evidence: current [Etsy tracker](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4494215556/perimenopause-symptom-tracker-printable)
  and [planner](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1293033073/menopause-journal-tracker-and-planner) offers.
- **Confidence/proof:** MEDIUM; externally evidenced, provisional. Need and paid
  channel exist; differentiation and medical-review cost are unverified.
- **Simplest buyable product:** A $12 clinician-reviewed PDF/printable containing
  a two-week log, one-page summary generator worksheet, appointment questions,
  and claim-evidence checklist; explicitly not medical advice.
- **Path to banked money:** Marketplace download purchase, processor settlement,
  then bank reconciliation to the exact order.
- **Strongest rejection:** Health-content liability and a saturated journal
  category; clinician review may make the tiny product uneconomic.
- **Fingerprint:** `menopause|appointment-brief|claim-literacy|non-diagnostic`
- **Origin:** `WEB-002` + `WEB-006`, hypothesis “trustworthy pre-appointment navigation rather than symptom-treatment promises.”

### `FSC-20260809-03` — Pet Relay Ready

- **Idea:** A waterproof handoff-card and expiry-label system so a pet sitter,
  neighbor, or family member can evacuate a pet with current medication,
  ownership, vet, carrier, and comfort instructions when the owner cannot.
- **Consumer/pain:** Pet owners who know they need an emergency kit but have
  scattered records, expired supplies, or nobody else able to act quickly.
- **Audience rationale:** Pet ownership is mainstream, yet official guidance is
  still checklist-shaped and leaves the handoff/refresh workflow to the owner.
  The narrow underserved job is keeping a second person ready, not merely owning
  a bag.
- **Evidence:** [FEMA 2025 pet-preparedness answer key](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_scenario_6_pet_preparedness_answer_key_06252025.pdf)
  calls for medication, carrier, vet contacts, and medical records;
  [CDC pet emergency checklist](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-pets/media/pdfs/disaster-prep-Pet-Emergency-Checklist-1.pdf)
  adds medical summary, ownership/adoption records, and medication instructions.
- **Confidence/proof:** LOW-MEDIUM; externally evidenced, provisional. The
  required job is authoritative, but `WEB-005` did not surface reliable sales or
  review counts for this exact offer.
- **Simplest buyable product:** A $16 mailed waterproof card/label pack plus
  printable duplicate stored with the carrier; no medication advice or cloud
  storage.
- **Path to banked money:** Small physical batch sold through a marketplace with
  existing pet traffic; bank success only after a genuine order settles and is
  reconciled, net of printing/postage.
- **Strongest rejection:** Free official checklists may be sufficient, and
  handling sensitive pet/owner records creates privacy responsibility.
- **Fingerprint:** `pet-emergency|delegated-handoff|expiry-labels|offline-records`
- **Origin:** `WEB-004` + failed channel attempt `WEB-005`, hypothesis “make pet evacuation executable by a backup human.”

- **Idea:** A guided physical/digital card deck that gets a family through one

  who are overwhelmed by duplicates, inconsistent organization, and the social
  work of deciding what matters.
- **Audience rationale:** Digital clutter is mainstream, but advanced photo
  managers focus on tooling. A bounded family ritual may serve consumers who do
  not want another subscription or a professional cataloging workflow.
- **Evidence:** [research on personal digital-file collections and duplication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06421);
  a 2025 survey reported [77% of respondents had more digital files than needed](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/digital-data-decluttering-could-save-you-hundreds-of-dollars-every-year-heres-how-you-can-do-it);
  current paid software can cost about $189 and photo-book services demonstrate
  spending on memory artifacts ([2026 comparison](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/buying-guides/best-photo-organizing-software),
  [photo-book comparison](https://www.techradar.com/news/best-photo-books)).
- **Confidence/proof:** LOW-MEDIUM; externally evidenced, provisional. The pain
  and adjacent spend are evidenced; the deck format and family ritual are not.
- **Simplest buyable product:** A $14 downloadable facilitator guide and card
  deck with a naming convention, duplicate triage rules, family-caption prompts,
  privacy warnings, and photo-book export checklist.
- **Path to banked money:** Digital marketplace sale first; optionally earn an
  affiliate/referral fee only if disclosed. Count success only after processor
  payout settles into the bank and reconciles to a real customer.
- **Strongest rejection:** Apple/Google photo tools already remove duplicates,
  and family coordination—not missing software—may be the true unsolved job.
- **Fingerprint:** `family-photos|bounded-declutter-ritual|shared-captions|print-exit`
- **Origin:** `WEB-001` + `WEB-008`, hypothesis “sell a bounded family decision ritual, not another photo manager.”

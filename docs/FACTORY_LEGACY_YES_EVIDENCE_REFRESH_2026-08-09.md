## Refreshed concept card

- **One-sentence idea:** A Bahrain-focused table finder that matches TTRPG and
  board-game players by game, schedule, language, experience, and open seats,
  then anchors the session at a known public venue such as The Tavern or The
  Raven’s Nest.
- **Consumer and job:** Adults who want an in-person game but lack enough
  compatible players, a GM, a reliable schedule, or confidence arriving alone.
- **Audience rationale:** The audience is niche but visibly active. A Manama
  board-game Meetup lists 81 members but no upcoming events, and recent Bahrain
  discussions still ask where to meet new board-game players. This suggests the
  local failure is coordination and table formation, not absence of venues.
- **Simplest usable product:** A mobile web app with public-venue-only locations,
  player and GM profiles, game/system tags, language and experience filters,
  scheduled tables, open-seat counts, join requests, waitlists, and reminders.
  No precise live location, home-game discovery, payments, or private messaging
  in the first validation version.
- **Credible bank path:** A participating venue pays a monthly table-filling fee
  or a disclosed fee is attached to a confirmed public-venue seat. Success is
  counted only after a genuine partner/customer payment settles into the bank
  and reconciles to the booking or subscription. Venue willingness to pay is
  currently unverified.
- **Strongest rejection:** Severe cold-start risk in a small geography. Global
  competitors are free, existing communities already coordinate through Meetup,
  Discord, WhatsApp, and Instagram, and both players and venues must participate
  before the matching product becomes better than a group chat.
- **Duplicate fingerprint:**
  `bahrain|tabletop-lfg|public-venue-tables|schedule-fit|open-seats`
- **Origin:** Legacy sponsor concept recovered from the supervising conversation;
  refreshed by attempts `WEB-LY-001` through `WEB-LY-006`.
- **Confidence/proof state:** **MEDIUM / externally evidenced, commercially
  UNVERIFIED.** Local venues and repeated group-finding demand are evidenced;
  retention, match density, venue cooperation, and payment are not.

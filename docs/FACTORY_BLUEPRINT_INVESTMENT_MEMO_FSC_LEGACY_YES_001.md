## Answer first

**Recommendation: REVISE the investment case; do not fund an app or catalog
from this Blueprint yet.** The original idea contains a real consumer outcome — finding a
welcoming table — but the broad matching-app thesis is not yet investable.
Bahrain already has several tabletop venues and events, and WhatsApp, Discord,
Meetup, Played Together, and Groupfinder already cover discovery, groups,
events, RSVP, reminders, or profiles. The defensible first offer is the outcome
they do not guarantee: an **18+ beginner-friendly, hosted TTRPG one-shot with
clear fit/safety rules and a confirmed seat**.

An original, system-agnostic **Reliable Table Kit** digital product remains a
useful adjacent hypothesis, but it serves a different global GM/host buyer,
channel, and geography. It is therefore a **separate speculative Factory
concept** that must pass its own Signal Scan and authority path; it cannot
inherit `FSC-LEGACY-YES-001` Blueprint authorization merely because some
components overlap.

No local willingness to pay, venue participation, host supply, conversion, or
settled revenue has been verified. No publishing, outreach, account creation,
spend, payment, or Assembly is authorized by this memo.

## The investment thesis

Start with a narrow paid outcome for adult newcomers who want to try a TTRPG
without recruiting a group or learning the rules alone; turn the original
operating assets into a low-support tabletop SKU family; build software only
after repeated paid matches make coordination the measured bottleneck.

## Three offers

### 1. Manual concierge pilot — recommended venture wedge

**Buyer:** adult newcomer or lapsed player.  
**Promise:** a beginner-friendly public-venue TTRPG one-shot with rules, table
expectations, and a confirmed seat handled.  
**Fulfilment:** one vetted host, one consenting public venue, 4–6 adult seats,
2.5–3 hours, boundaries, waitlist/replacement SOP, and refund/issue path.  
**Price test:** BHD5 local anchor; BHD7 upside hypothesis. BHD9 is retained only
as a sensitivity cell, not a recommended first price.  
**Cuts:** no subscriptions, homes, minors, live location, public profiles, open
marketplace, direct messages, or app.

The revised model holds operations at a conservative two hours, varies price
and seats independently, and treats host compensation as the causal variable.
With a fixed BHD30 host, 3% payment cost, 5% refund reserve, and operations
valued at BHD5/hour, contribution is:

| Price / paid seats | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BHD5 | -21.60 | -17.00 | -12.40 | -3.20 | 6.00 |
| BHD7 | -14.24 | -7.80 | -1.36 | 11.52 | 24.40 |
| BHD9 | -6.88 | 1.40 | 9.68 | 26.24 | 42.80 |

At the only observed local BHD5 D&D anchor, a normal 4–6 seat table loses money
under the BHD30 host assumption. Six BHD5 seats permit at most **BHD17.60 host
compensation** after the modeled fees/refunds and operations labor. A 70% host
revenue share still loses BHD3.40 at six BHD5 seats and clears only BHD1 at ten
seats; a ten-player table may damage the promised experience. The first live
test must therefore validate host terms and buyer price, not presume margin.

### 2. Lightweight membership/event matching — defer

Possible BHD3–5/month priority invitations or hosted-seat credit. Defer until
recurring inventory and retention exist; free substitutes are strong. A
subscription before four successful paid sessions tests a promise, not service.

### 3. Software path — conditional

Use form + spreadsheet + manually generated group brief + private ledger first.
Software is warranted only after four paid sessions, repeat behavior, and manual
coordination above 60 minutes/session or a measured coordination failure. It
should automate intake, confirmation, replacement, or host operations—not chat.

### SKU

Use 90 days after separately approved publishing. Keep a follow-on at ≥9 settled

### 3. **Enhanced Controls**
- **Tap any song** → Plays specific track
- **Tap any mood** → Plays mood playlist
- **Quick Play Button** → Starts energetic mix
- **Voice Feedback** → Pi confirms with audio response

## 🎮 **How to Use:**

### **Play Specific Songs:**
1. Tap **♪ Music** button
2. Choose from **🎶 Individual Songs**
3. Tap any track to play instantly
4. Pi responds: *"Now playing your selected song through Bluetooth speaker!"*

### **Play Mood Playlists:**
1. Tap **♪ Music** button  
2. Choose from **🎭 Mood Playlists**
3. Tap any mood (Energetic, Relaxing, etc.)
4. Pi responds: *"Starting [mood] music playlist! Perfect choice for your current mood."*

### **Quick Controls:**
- **🎵 Play Energetic Mix** → Instant party mode
- **Shuffle** → Random playlist order
- **Stop** → Pause current music
- **Next** → Skip to next track

## 🔊 **Audio Features:**

### **Bluetooth Integration:**
- All music plays through your Pi's connected Bluetooth speaker
- High-quality audio generated by Pi using sox synthesis
- No external music files needed - Pi creates everything!

### **Voice Confirmations:**
- Pi speaks back to confirm every music command
- Personalized responses for different actions
- Audio feedback helps you know commands worked

### **Visual Feedback:**
- ✅ Green notifications for successful commands
- ❌ Red notifications for failed commands
- Real-time status updates in the app

## 🎼 **Music Generation:**
Your Pi creates all tracks automatically:
- **Electronic**: Sine waves with tremolo effects
- **Ambient**: Layered tones with reverb
- **Nature**: Synthesized birds/water sounds
- **Classical**: Piano-like pluck synthesis
- **Chiptune**: 8-bit square waves
- **Lo-fi**: Mixed sine waves with noise

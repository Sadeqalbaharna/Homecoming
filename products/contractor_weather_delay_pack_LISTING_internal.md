## Full listing draft

Weather delays are annoying. Proving them later is worse.

This pack gives small contractors a simple way to document weather impacts the same day they happen:

1. record the weather event,
2. log what work was affected,
3. track crew and equipment impact,
4. index the photos/videos,
5. track the notice deadline,
6. send a clear written notice.

No macros. No subscriptions. No giant platform to learn while the site is already soaked.

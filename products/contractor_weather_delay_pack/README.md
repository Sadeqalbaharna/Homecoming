# Small Contractor Weather-Delay Evidence Log + Delay Notice Pack

A practical document pack for small contractors who need to record weather impacts clearly and send timely delay notices before the paper trail disappears.

This is not legal advice. It is an operations/evidence pack: structured logs, checklists, and editable notice templates designed to help a contractor document what happened, when it happened, who was affected, and what notice was sent.

---

## What is included

### Workbook templates

Open the CSV files in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers, or LibreOffice.

1. `workbook/daily_weather_delay_log.csv`
   - Daily weather conditions
   - Affected work areas
   - Crew/equipment impact
   - Lost hours
   - Photo references
   - Contract notice deadline
   - Notice status

2. `workbook/weather_photo_evidence_index.csv`
   - Photo/video filename
   - Date/time captured
   - Location
   - What it proves
   - Linked delay log ID

3. `workbook/delay_notice_tracker.csv`
   - Event ID
   - Notice due date
   - Recipient
   - Delivery method
   - Sent date/time
   - Follow-up status

### Editable templates

4. `templates/weather_delay_notice_template.md`
   - Short written notice of weather delay / impact
   - Includes fields for contract section, impacted work, mitigation, and reservation of rights

5. `templates/notice_of_impact_template.md`
   - Broader notice for weather-related impact when the full schedule effect is not yet known

6. `templates/weather_delay_daily_report_insert.md`
   - Copy/paste block for daily reports

7. `templates/photo_evidence_checklist.md`
   - Simple checklist for collecting useful site photos/videos

8. `templates/quick_start.md`
   - How to use the pack in under 10 minutes

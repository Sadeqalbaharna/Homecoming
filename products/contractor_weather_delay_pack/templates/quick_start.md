# Quick Start — Use This Pack in 10 Minutes

## 1. Create a project folder

Make one folder for the project, for example:

`Project Name / Weather Delay Evidence /`

Inside it, keep:

- the three workbook CSVs
- weather photos/videos
- copies of sent notices
- proof of delivery

## 2. Start the daily log

Open `workbook/daily_weather_delay_log.csv` in Excel or Google Sheets.

For each weather event, create one delay event ID:

`WD-001`, `WD-002`, `WD-003`, etc.

## 3. Index your photos

Every useful photo/video gets a photo ID:

`PHOTO-001`, `PHOTO-002`, `PHOTO-003`, etc.

Log those in `workbook/weather_photo_evidence_index.csv` and link them back to the delay event ID.

## 4. Track notice deadlines

Open `workbook/delay_notice_tracker.csv`.

For each event, record:

- notice deadline
- who must receive notice
- how notice must be sent
- when it was actually sent
- where proof of delivery is saved

## 5. Send the right notice

Use:

- `weather_delay_notice_template.md` when you are documenting a clear weather delay
- `notice_of_impact_template.md` when the full schedule/cost impact is not known yet

## 6. Keep the disclaimer

This pack helps organize documentation. It does not replace your contract, legal advice, or jurisdiction-specific notice rules.
